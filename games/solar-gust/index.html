Solar Gust

You are the wind inside a star. Fuse hydrogen into heavier and heavier elements.

  • ← ↑ ↓ →
    Swipe, press an arrow key or click a side of the field. Every atom is blown as far as it can go.
  • 3 or more of the same atom that touch fuse into the next element: hydrogen makes helium, helium makes lithium, and so on.
  • The new atom can fuse again straight away. Set up a chain for ×2, ×3 and more.
  • The faint atom shows what appears after your next gust. Blow an atom onto it and it appears somewhere else.
  • +2
    Fuse 5 or more at once to skip an element. Can you reach argon, number 18? The star dies when the field is full.
